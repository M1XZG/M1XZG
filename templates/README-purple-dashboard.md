## Hello

I'm Rob. I work at [GitHub](https://github.com), drink an unreasonable amount of coffee, and wish pumpkin spice lattes were available all year.

Away from work, I tinker with Bash and Zsh scripts, home automation, and a small fleet of Raspberry Pis. A Pi 4 with 8 GB of RAM runs Docker containers and Plex at home. I also run my own [Raspberry Pi kiosk configuration](https://github.com/M1XZG/Raspberry-pi-kiosk-config) and [ADS-B reception, decoding and sharing with Docker](https://sdr-enthusiasts.gitbook.io/ads-b/).
