## On this frequency

I'm Rob. I work at [GitHub](https://github.com) :octocat:, drink a frankly unreasonable amount of coffee, and still wish [pumpkin spice lattes](https://www.starbucks.com/menu/product/418/hot) were sold all year.

Away from work, I'm usually writing Bash or Zsh scripts, adjusting home automation, or finding another job for a Raspberry Pi. A Pi 4 with 8 GB of RAM runs Docker containers and Plex at home. I also maintain my own [Raspberry Pi kiosk configuration](https://github.com/M1XZG/Raspberry-pi-kiosk-config) and run [ADS-B reception, decoding and sharing with Docker](https://sdr-enthusiasts.gitbook.io/ads-b/).

A few projects and organisations I keep coming back to:

- [Discord bot for fun](https://github.com/M1XZG/discord-bot-for-fun), complete with fishing and a fishing contest mode
- [Swindon Makerspace](https://github.com/swindonmakers)
- [Oh My Zsh](https://github.com/ohmyzsh/ohmyzsh) and [ZSH History](https://github.com/rchakra3/zsh_history)
- [TTS Voice Wizard](https://github.com/VRCWizard/TTS-Voice-Wizard), which I use in [VRChat](https://vrchat.com/)
